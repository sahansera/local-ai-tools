# Governance

Local AI Tools begins with a lightweight maintainer-led governance model.

## Maintainer

Sahan Serasinghe (`@sahansera`) is the initial project maintainer and final decision maker while the project is in its early stages.

## Principles

- Prefer simple, low-maintenance architecture.
- Keep catalogue metadata factual and auditable.
- Keep listed third-party projects independent.
- Avoid automatic execution or installation of untrusted third-party code by default.
- Make significant architectural changes visible through issues or pull requests.

## Adding maintainers

Additional maintainers may be invited based on sustained, constructive contributions and demonstrated judgment in the relevant project area.

## Decision making

Routine changes are decided through normal pull-request review. Significant or irreversible changes should document alternatives and rationale before implementation.
